Started in YYCHacks 2025, CashCorn makes it harder for you to impulsively spend by popping up an annoying squirrel game on your most frequented sites when you click the 'Buy Now' button. We made the Chrome extension using TypeScript and Tailwind CSS and trained our own model to predict whether the URL you're currently visiting has a risk for you to impulsively spend on, based on your financial behaviors. Developed in under 18 hours, got third place.
