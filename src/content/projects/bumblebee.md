A moonshot project refers to an ambitious, exploratory, and groundbreaking initiative with high risk but equally high potential impact. My partner and I were exploring the sustainable energy space when we decided to deep dive into the world of wireless electricity. Bumblee was a pivot from a larger-scale idea of implementing a completely wireless energy grid by utilizing MRC (Magnetic Resonance Coupling) technology and enhancing its efficiency with radio wave energy harvesters. Our thinking sought to solve the issue of vulnerable physical infrastructure affecting access to electricity in remote regions and areas impacted by severe natural disasters. We pivoted to the Bumblee battery pack after understanding the associated risks of a completely wireless grid (although we are not far from a future where this exists). Instead, we implemented our previous system—without coupling—on a smaller scale, where energy is supplied through photovoltaic technology and radio wave energy harvesters.
