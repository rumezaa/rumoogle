Inspired by generational communication between parents and children, we created a tool that allows real-time monitoring of your communication with family members, partners, or friends. The tool helps assess how they'll receive your message and suggests the best way to communicate based on their attitudes. Developed prototype in <24 hours using React JS, TailwindCSS, Python, and Open AI API. This app was developed for Albert Health Hacks.
