I was one of the 32 people selected to participate in the Hunger Games-style Cohere x Open Sesame Hackathon in Toronto. I flew out for the sole purpose of attending because hackathons make me feel things. We created an AI tutor using the Tavus API that you can join a call with. Simply upload a document ranging from complicated math problems to 2025 mood boards and have a real-time conversation with our assistant.
