Meetmidway started at the Google Gemini hackathon and then refused to stay a hackathon project — we compressed what was scoped as two months of work into two weeks of grind. The problem is one every friend group has: you spend more time negotiating where to meet than actually meeting. So we built something that takes everyone’s address and preferences and hands back a finished itinerary.
