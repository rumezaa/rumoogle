Our platform onboard vulnerable individuals by listening to their stories and struggles. We use a custom TensorFlow model with 75 input nodes and dual 16-node hidden layers to analyze keyword frequency and emotions in user narratives. This allows us to generate tailored resource, job, and mentor suggestions based on users' experiences and skills. Developed in under 24 hours for Hack the Change 2024, we also engaged with our target demographic (the homeless) to gather feedback on our product.
