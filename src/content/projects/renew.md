I created Renew, a platform that organizes the allocation of excess donations to charities and non-profits. My research in the fashion sustainability space and interviews with niche non-profits revealed that 80% of clothing donations are exported overseas to developing countries, rather than being distributed within our own economy to help scale local charities, non-profits, or aid people in need. I developed a mobile app solution to address this issue by creating a platform that organizes the allocation of excess donations to charities and non-profits. I developed the platform in a week using React Native, Firebase, and Nativewind CSS. I developed a Calendly variation from scratch for scheduling functionality in the app, a direct messaging system, and a browsing page. I have partnered with the local non-profit Good-Neighbor, planning to utilize the app to increase their garment supply and reach more people in need.
