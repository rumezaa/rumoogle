After a few calls and a LinkedIn message from a girl that's become one of my closest friends, Ascend Calgary took form in a few short weeks. Ascend is Calgary's extension of Socratica but we've become so much more than that. Over the 6 months I've watched this community evolve into something that I wish I had when I was starting in my career. Not just a place, a home for my craziest ideas and a space to be vulnerable with the things I create. We've hosted 30 sessions by the time I'm posting this and we're looking to set up our demo day symposium called the Rodeo in a few short weeks.
