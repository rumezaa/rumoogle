In the 2024-2025 year I started university, but I also started feeling more lost than ever. I saw everyone around me fighting for the same internships, the same medallions, and I couldn’t help but wonder why. Why do I want this? Where will it take me? Why is this important to me?

Along the way I lost a sense of who I wanted to become and started getting sucked into who I should be, based off of the things I saw in the people around me. So I escaped for a bit — outside of software. I took trips. I started increasing my surface area for luck. And I started to remember what life could be, how individual it gets the second you focus on the type of story you want to create for yourself.

When I came back home from my adventures, I wanted to bring that same sense of adventure and journey of ‘becoming’ to the people around me. To give them the choice to break free of predisposed routines and start leading a life they actually want.

I created the Archive in two weeks, directed a film for our launch that gained hundreds of thousands of views, and used $6k of my own internship money to fund 8 sidequests around the world.

Was a super fun and different experience, especially for someone that's in a completely separate space - was a good run, and showed me whats possible when you want something bad enough :)
