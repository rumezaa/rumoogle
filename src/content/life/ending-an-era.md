Put a track athlete on a track, and they'll run relative to their peers. When you're running with fast people, you run faster.

From the ages of 16-18, with confidence, I can say I was running fast. All I had around me were people who were running faster. That pushed me in many ways to see what I could become if I moved farther than I thought I could.

That hasn't changed since I started university, but my track moved a bit. I've been so privileged to have surrounded myself with the most creative individuals I've ever met these past few months. The most human ones. Naturally, as with the growing company of creatives, some of my interests began to shift too.

This came in the form of taking time off of my technical pursuits to focus on building a philanthropic grant, in which I gave five grand of my own money to send people on adventures all over the world. Hosting events, most recently of which I hosted a 600 person builder film festival (The Canon Event).

All of these experiences, coupled, showed me a different direction I could take my life, my passions and my interests. I know I have always wanted to build things for people, but for a time I was curating for people instead, and that certainly has its own very critical impact.

After we all took that bow November 15 on the stage for the Canon Event, I knew that I would be saying goodbye to this phase of my life. The weeks that led up to the event showed me the sheer amount of determination, delusion and importance of a good freaking team it takes to put something up at scale.* All throughout the time I was putting this and The Archive cohort together, I realized I wanted to be on the other end. I don't want to be the one curating anymore. I want to be the one building. I miss building for people. I miss stressing about my API limits and the sheer amount of time I'd spend trying to make something work.

For a while I was running track with people on a different track. A wonderful, important track, but I realize I want to belong elsewhere. I just have to create that belonging and start running the race I wanted to be a part of at the start.

So that's what I'm doing next: building again. More on that soon.
